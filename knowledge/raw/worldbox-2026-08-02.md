## Relevant claims on the inspected page

The page describes a pixel-art sandbox where the observer can create life and
watch it prosper. Creatures and civilizations build houses and roads, evolve,
and go to war. It emphasizes watching evolution and the rise of civilizations
inside one visible world, along with terrain and disaster simulation.

## Interpretation for Universe

Retain the readability and pleasure of watching many autonomous actors in one
pixel world. Do not inherit the reference's small-map assumption, shallow
civilization model, god-power interaction loop, or manual spawning. Universe
must begin from a configured physical zero state and create life through its
own deterministic simulation.

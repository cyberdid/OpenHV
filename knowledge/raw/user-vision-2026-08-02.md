## Required outcome

The entire project must move to one platform, selected by the implementation
team. No existing function may be removed to make the merge easier.

The user is an observer only. The system must not require faction selection,
cell selection, battle launch, building orders, diplomacy choices, or any
other intervention. The world begins from zero and runs autonomously.

The product has three conceptual stages:

1. Planetary physics creates climate and conditions that drive ecosystems and
   all living development.
2. Life appears, a race emerges, civilization develops from prehistory through
   a maximally technological Imperium, then becomes space-capable.
3. Space travel connects planets and races through ships, frigates, flotillas,
   colonization, trade, diplomacy, conflict, and invasion.

The first implementation contains one complete planet, but the architecture
must support three planets. Each planet ultimately produces a distinct native
race. The separately developed Tyranid race later invades the first planet.

The desired view combines:

- WorldBox-like visible life, settlements, borders, movement, and conflict;
- Civilization/OpenCiv-like whole-world readability and deep civil systems;
- continuous real time instead of turns;
- RTS detail for warfare without moving to a separate arena or losing the
  global world context.

The map must represent a large planet rather than WorldBox's small sandbox.
Economy, politics, diplomacy, trade, technology, demographics, ecology, and
warfare all require explicit detailed logic.

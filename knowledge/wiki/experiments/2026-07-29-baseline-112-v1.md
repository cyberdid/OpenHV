# Civil and Military Baseline 112 v1

## Purpose

Complete SIM-010 with the first held-out, statistically useful view of how the
four military profiles affect both RTS performance and faction life. This is a
descriptive baseline, not a tuning contest: it asks what the current system
does, where map sensitivity dominates, whether intended identities are
visible, and which guardrails fail before AI-003–004.

Compact immutable evidence is split into:

- [run provenance](../../raw/experiments/2026-07-29-baseline-112-v1-run.csv);
- [profile and profile-map metrics](../../raw/experiments/2026-07-29-baseline-112-v1-metrics.csv);
- [paired profile differences](../../raw/experiments/2026-07-29-baseline-112-v1-paired.csv).

Full local artifacts are at
`/Users/helenshkirenko/Universe/simulation-runs/baseline-112-v1`.

## Design

The generated schedule contains 112 four-way FFA matches:

- four maps: Cold Rage, Doubles, Tournament Island, and Yet Another Beautiful
  Winter;
- four cyclic mappings of Aggressor, Economist, Technologist, and Fortress to
  the four lobby slots;
- seven independent held-out seeds per map/slot cell;
- one of every profile in every match, producing 112 observations per profile
  and 448 player observations;
- 12,000 synchronized ticks, 1,000-tick telemetry, balanced civil profile,
  trade enabled, advisory-only 2,000-tick stalemate window;
- four isolated workers and one retained replay per map.

Every profile occupies every lobby slot exactly seven times on each map.
OpenRA still randomizes spawn and faction from the recorded seed, so the
analyzer reports those realized distributions rather than pretending the
generator directly assigned them.

The simulation used clean commit
`3308752a5c8f4a340b05d416f09b8ffc8ced2d3e`; schedule hash
`87df1f0513b4da6c9516bf64ce8c6f2f4167d70dad78a9a593ed6b90eb71e3bf`.

```sh
python3 run-batch.py batch-manifests/baseline-112-v1.json
python3 analyze-baseline.py \
  ../simulation-runs/baseline-112-v1
```

The analyzer requires one clean commit, a complete batch, and exactly one of
each profile per result. Reported score-lead rates use Wilson 95% intervals.
Continuous means use a deterministic 2,000-resample nonparametric bootstrap.
Within-match profile differences are paired before bootstrapping.

## Execution and artifact integrity

All 112 matches completed on attempt 1 in 522.827 wall seconds. There were no
retry, crash, desync, watchdog, invalid result, non-empty stderr, or
infrastructure failure artifacts.

QA validated:

- 112 Result Schema v1 documents;
- 1,456 Telemetry Schema v1 snapshots;
- 8,574 Event Schema v1 records;
- one clean Git commit across every result;
- four retained replays, each readable by `--replay-metadata` with
  `FinalGameTick=12000`.

The event corpus contains 1,475 diplomacy transitions, 1,480 route-state
transitions, 23 shipments, 1,751 strategy transitions, 439 technology
completions, 2,716 population changes, and 15 faction-collapse events.

## Scenario outcome: a failed natural-completion guardrail

Every match reached `world-tick-limit`; none produced a natural winner or
stalemate. Therefore the timeout/cutoff rate is 100%, far above the provisional
below-10% guardrail. The score leader is only the leader at the declared
12,000-tick observation and is never interpreted as a combat winner.

This is a useful negative result. Four simulated minutes are enough for civil
research, war, peace, casualties, base destruction, and recovery, but the
current tactical AI does not reliably finish the remaining opponents. AI-004
needs explicit finishing-target and regroup/retreat behavior, followed by a
longer natural-outcome fixture.

Timed score leads were Fortress 32, Economist 31, Aggressor 29, and
Technologist 20. Their Wilson intervals overlap, so lead counts alone do not
establish dominance. Paired score means are more informative:

- Fortress exceeded Aggressor by 14,681
  (bootstrap 95%: 5,780 to 23,356);
- Fortress exceeded Technologist by 14,693
  (6,883 to 21,970);
- Economist exceeded Aggressor by 10,997
  (1,055 to 20,738);
- Economist exceeded Technologist by 11,009
  (2,165 to 19,881);
- Fortress versus Economist and Aggressor versus Technologist remained
  inconclusive.

## Profile outcomes

| Profile | Score leads | Mean score | Population | Stability | Army value | Casualties | Collapses |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Aggressor | 29 | 67,187 | 897.7 | 853.0 | 8,316 | 16.6 | 12 |
| Economist | 31 | 78,184 | 1,006.8 | 824.6 | 14,342 | 17.2 | 0 |
| Technologist | 20 | 67,175 | 980.6 | 786.4 | 11,925 | 25.2 | 2 |
| Fortress | 32 | 81,869 | 1,030.7 | 923.1 | 17,445 | 11.0 | 1 |

Fortress is the strongest current all-round survivor. It combined the highest
mean score, population, prosperity, stability, army/assets, and lowest
casualties. Relative to Technologist, paired mean stability was 137 points
higher and casualties 14.2 lower; both bootstrap intervals excluded zero.

Aggressor is a high-damage but fragile glass cannon. It dealt the most kill
value (19,054 mean) but retained the smallest army, lost 12 civil societies,
and had population 133 below Fortress in paired comparison. Fourteen of all
15 collapse events were civil-threshold classifications and one was an engine
loss; the collapsed factions had no surviving settlement population even when
OpenRA still left other assets and an undefined outcome.

Economist remained demographically stable with no collapse, but its final
behavior does not match a peaceful trading specialist: 111/112 observations
ended in `mobilization`, it averaged 159.8 mobilized adults, and it remained in
about one active war. It scored well through assets/army rather than through
observed trade.

Technologist did not produce a technology advantage. Every profile averaged
about one completed technology; Technologist instead had the lowest stability,
highest casualties, most active wars, and only 20 timed leads. Its current
tactical production weights and Civilization AI research preference are not
yet coupled into a distinct technology path.

## Map sensitivity

Doubles is the critical Aggressor failure surface: nine of its 12 collapses
occurred there, mean population fell to 678, and it led only 2/28 observations.
The same profile led 16/28 on Cold Rage with mean population 936. This is too
large to summarize as a single global profile number.

Fortress led 8–10 observations on Doubles, Tournament Island, and Winter, with
mean stability 920–943 on those maps. Economist led 10/28 on both Doubles and
Tournament Island. Technologist improved from 1/28 leads on Cold Rage to 8/28
on Doubles, but its casualties and low stability persisted.

Faction and spawn counts were not perfectly equal because they were
deterministically randomized by OpenRA. The analysis artifact therefore keeps
full profile×faction and profile×spawn distributions. Candidate evaluation
must reuse this exact schedule so those realized effects cancel in paired
comparisons.

## Trade and research limits

Balanced-profile factions were mostly self-sufficient: only 23 shipments
occurred across 112 matches; total trade volume had median and 90th percentile
zero. This does not contradict the asymmetric trade experiment, where real
deficits generated 750 units of exchange. It shows that the baseline cannot
measure trade identity until AI-003 creates specialization or a controlled
scarcity/trade schedule.

Research also converged to one early unlock for almost everyone. AI-003 must
connect strategy to opening, economy, and technology choice; otherwise the
named profiles are mainly tactical weight presets plus different exposure to
the same diplomacy loop.

## Decision

SIM-010 is complete as a reproducible descriptive baseline, while its
natural-completion guardrail explicitly fails. The evidence prioritizes:

1. AI-003 opening/economy/technology planners that create measurable
   specialization and keep crisis strategy from collapsing into permanent
   mobilization;
2. AI-004 finishing targets, retreat, regroup, and re-engagement so conflicts
   can reach natural outcomes;
3. exact-schedule candidate comparisons against this committed baseline;
4. a separate asymmetric scarcity/trade suite rather than claiming balanced
   FFA measures trade quality.

No current score lead is promoted to a balance winner.
